# Xinzhi (Berry) Liu

Power electronics engineer working on GaN and SiC device reliability at circuit level. I build the test hardware, gate drivers and measurement workflows that show how wide bandgap devices behave, degrade and fail under real converter conditions, and use SPICE and TCAD to explain what the measurements show.

Postdoctoral Research Fellow, School of Engineering, University of Warwick, UK.

### What I work on

- **Converter-level test platforms:** third-quadrant reverse conduction, dynamic R<sub>DS(on)</sub>, multi-pulse C<sub>oss</sub> extraction, short circuit, UIS and surge testing
- **Devices:** single and dual gate bidirectional GaN switches, Schottky p-GaN HEMTs, GITs, SiC MOSFETs (40 V to 1200 V)
- **Hardware:** Altium PCB design, isolated Kelvin-referenced gate drivers, low-inductance power loops, STM32 pulse timing firmware
- **Modelling:** LTspice electro-thermal models, Sentaurus TCAD
- **Data:** Python and PowerShell for instrument control and large waveform datasets
